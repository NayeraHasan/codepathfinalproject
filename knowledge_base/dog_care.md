# Dog Care Guide

## Feeding Guidelines

Adult dogs should be fed 2–3 times per day at consistent times to reinforce routine. Puppies under 6 months need 3–4 small meals daily. Senior dogs (7+ years) benefit from smaller, more frequent meals to ease digestion.

Recommended feeding schedule:
- Morning feeding: 7:00 AM – 8:00 AM
- Midday (optional for adults): 12:00 PM
- Evening feeding: 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Always provide fresh water alongside meals. Avoid feeding large breeds immediately before or after vigorous exercise to reduce the risk of bloat (gastric dilatation-volvulus).

## Exercise Requirements

Daily exercise is essential for physical and mental health:
- Small breeds (Chihuahua, Pomeranian): 20–30 minutes/day
- Medium breeds (Beagle, Cocker Spaniel): 30–60 minutes/day
- Large breeds (Labrador, German Shepherd): 60–90 minutes/day

Distribute activity across morning and evening walks. Allow at least 30 minutes after meals before vigorous exercise.

## Medications and Health

- **Heartworm prevention**: Monthly oral tablet; give on the same calendar day each month, with food to reduce stomach upset.
- **Flea and tick prevention**: Monthly oral (NexGard, Simparica) or topical (Frontline, Revolution) treatment.
- **Vaccines**: DHPP core vaccine every 1–3 years after the puppy series; rabies per local law.
- **Dental care**: Brush 2–3 times per week; professional cleaning annually.

Never give human medications (ibuprofen, acetaminophen, xylitol-containing products) to dogs — many are toxic.

## Grooming

- Short-haired breeds: brush weekly.
- Long-haired breeds: brush daily or every other day.
- Bathing: every 4–6 weeks or as needed.
- Nail trimming: every 3–4 weeks; overgrown nails can affect gait.

## Warning Signs — Seek Veterinary Care

- Loss of appetite for more than 24 hours
- Lethargy or sudden behavior change
- Vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than 24 hours
- Difficulty breathing, walking, or standing
- Bloated or distended abdomen
